I visit a lot of cafes that buy our beans. When the owner says the beans are bad, half the time it is not the beans.

It is the morning routine. The first shot is sour, so the grind moves a big step finer. The next one is bitter, so it moves back. By ten o'clock half a bag is in the bin.

What I do on every visit takes 15 minutes. One part of it: after any grind change, grind and throw away one dose before the next shot. The old grounds sitting in the chute go into the next shot otherwise, and you end up judging the setting you just left.
