# Voice note transcript (owner of Acme Coffee, invented)

So, the thing I keep seeing when I visit cafes that buy from us. Half the time when they say the beans are bad, it's not the beans. They change the grind in the morning because the first shot tastes sour, then the next one is bitter, and by ten o'clock they've thrown away maybe half a bag. Nobody weighs the dose. They eyeball it. And the grinder hasn't been cleaned in a month.

What I do when I visit is always the same. I weigh the dose, eighteen grams for a double. I weigh the shot in the cup, thirty-six grams out. I time it, I want it somewhere around twenty-five to thirty seconds. If it's fast I go finer, if it's slow I go coarser, one step at a time, and I throw away the first shot after any grind change because the old grounds are still in the chute. Fifteen minutes and the shots taste the same every time.

Then the other stuff. Water temperature, the machine should be warm for at least twenty minutes. Clean the group head, backflush. Beans should be rested, at least a week after roast for espresso, and used within about a month. Store them sealed, not in the hopper overnight.

What we sell is the wholesale subscription. They get beans on a schedule so they never run out or sit on stale stock, and I come out and dial in the first bag with them. The people who struggle with dial-in are exactly the people who'd want that.

I'd want them to comment something on Instagram and get the checklist in their DMs. Maybe "dial in"?
