# Dial in your espresso in 15 minutes: 22 checks for small cafes

> ☕ For cafe owners and head baristas with one machine and beans bought by the bag. In 15 minutes you will have a shot that tastes the same as yesterday's, and a list of what to fix this week.

When we asked 24 independent cafe owners about their biggest challenge, 10 wrote about shots that taste different from one morning to the next. Source: Acme Coffee customer survey, 2026 (invented example data).

Most of the time it is not the beans. It is an unweighed dose, a grind changed in big jumps, and old grounds sitting in the grinder.

## Start here: the 5 checks that fix most mornings

Do these five in order before opening. They take about 15 minutes and need a scale that reads 0.1 g and a timer.

- [ ] **Weigh the dose every time.** Why: a 1 g swing changes the shot more than most grind changes. Check: 18.0 g in the basket for a double (or your recipe), on the scale, every shot.
- [ ] **Weigh the shot in the cup.** Why: volume lies, crema makes shots look bigger. Check: the scale under the cup reads 36 g when you stop (a 1:2 ratio for an 18 g dose).
- [ ] **Time from pump on to pump off.** Why: time tells you which way to move the grind. Check: 25 to 30 seconds for the 36 g.
- [ ] **Move the grind one step at a time.** Why: big jumps overshoot and you chase the shot all morning. Check: fast and sour means one step finer; slow and bitter means one step coarser.
- [ ] **Purge after every grind change.** Why: the old grounds in the chute go into the next shot. Check: grind and throw away about one dose (or the amount your grinder holds) before the next shot.

## Before the first shot

- [ ] **Machine warm for at least 20 minutes.** Why: a cold group head pulls sour shots even with the right grind. Check: the group head is hot to the touch and the boiler light has cycled.
- [ ] **Flush the group head.** Why: it clears old water and stabilises temperature. Check: run water for 3 seconds before locking in the portafilter.
- [ ] **Portafilter dry and clean.** Why: water and old coffee in the basket make uneven shots. Check: wipe the basket dry before every dose.
- [ ] **Beans rested after roast.** Why: very fresh beans release gas and pull wild shots. Check: the roast date on the bag is at least 7 days ago for espresso.
- [ ] **Beans used within about 30 days of roast.** Why: stale beans taste flat and pull fast. Check: the roast date is less than about a month ago.
- [ ] **Only today's beans in the hopper.** Why: beans left in the hopper overnight go stale faster. Check: the hopper holds roughly one day's use; the rest stays in a sealed bag.

## Pulling the shot

- [ ] **Even distribution before tamping.** Why: clumps make channels and uneven taste. Check: the bed looks level before you tamp.
- [ ] **Level tamp, same pressure each time.** Why: a tilted tamp lets water run down one side. Check: the coffee surface is flat and the tamper sits level.
- [ ] **Lock in and start straight away.** Why: a hot group head starts cooking the coffee before the shot. Check: under 5 seconds between locking in and pressing start.
- [ ] **Taste every dial-in shot.** Why: numbers get you close, taste confirms. Check: sour means under-extracted (finer), bitter or dry means over-extracted (coarser).
- [ ] **Write down the settings that worked.** Why: tomorrow starts from today's answer, not from zero. Check: grind setting, dose, yield and time on a card by the grinder.

## During the day

- [ ] **Re-check after the morning rush.** Why: grinders warm up and the shot speeds up. Check: weigh and time one shot around midday, adjust one step if it drifted.
- [ ] **Same recipe for every barista on shift.** Why: three people pulling three recipes means three different coffees. Check: the card by the grinder is the recipe everyone uses.
- [ ] **New bag, quick re-check.** Why: each bag can need a small grind change. Check: weigh and time the first shot from a new bag.

## Weekly

- [ ] **Clean the grinder burrs and chute.** Why: old oily grounds taste bitter and stale. Check: no oily residue on the burrs or in the chute.
- [ ] **Backflush the group heads with cleaner.** Why: old oils build up in the shower screen. Check: the water runs clear after the cleaning cycle.
- [ ] **Check the shower screen and gasket.** Why: a worn gasket leaks and a blocked screen sprays unevenly. Check: no coffee on the outside of the portafilter after a shot.

## Mistakes to avoid

- **Changing dose and grind at the same time.** Fix: change one thing per shot, so you know which change worked.
- **Judging the shot by colour or volume.** Fix: weigh and time it, then taste.
- **Blaming the beans first.** Fix: run the 5 checks under Start here before you change the coffee.
- **Keeping the hopper full overnight.** Fix: fill it for the day, keep the rest sealed.
