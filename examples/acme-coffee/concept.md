# Concept card: Dial in your espresso in 15 minutes: 22 checks for small cafes

| | |
|---|---|
| Reader | Owner or head barista of an independent cafe with 1 to 3 staff, one espresso machine, beans bought per bag |
| Pain, in their words | "My shots taste different every morning and I end up changing the grind five times before opening." |
| Outcome | Shots that taste the same every morning, dialled in within 15 minutes, with less coffee thrown away |
| Paid offer | Acme wholesale bean subscription: beans on a schedule, first bag dialled in on site |
| Bridge | The checklist shows the owner where dial-in breaks. Many gaps come from stale or uneven stock and no help on the first bag, which the subscription fixes |
| Type | reveal-problem |
| Format | checklist (22 items) |
| Delivery | page (HTML, also pushed to Notion) |
| Quick win | The 5 checks under "Start here": weigh dose, weigh yield, time the shot, one grind step at a time, purge after a change. About 15 minutes |
| Hook fact | Owner's visit notes: "half the time when they say the beans are bad, it's not the beans". No sourced number, so the hook uses the survey count (10 of 24 answers) |
| CTA | keyword DIALIN |
| Scores | specific 5, bridge 4, quick win 5, value 4, AI-proof 3 = 29/35 |

## Title options
1. Dial in your espresso in 15 minutes: 22 checks for small cafes  <- picked
2. Same-tasting shots every morning: a 22-point dial-in checklist
3. Stop throwing away half a bag before opening: 22 dial-in checks

## What only we have
- The owner's exact visit routine, in the order he does it
- Survey answers from 24 cafe owners, in their words
- The mistakes he sees on visits (no scale, dirty grinder, beans in the hopper overnight)

## Rejected concepts
- Weekly bean order calculator: tied at 29, kept as magnet 2 (needs a small build)
- Barista training routine: weak bridge to the offer, quick win too long
- Complete guide to coffee origins: not specific, no quick win, a chatbot answers it
