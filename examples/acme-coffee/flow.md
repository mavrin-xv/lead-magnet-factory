# Delivery flow: Dial in your espresso in 15 minutes: 22 checks for small cafes

Platform: instagram. Keyword: **DIALIN**. Magnet: https://example.com/dial-in

## Checks

- PASS keyword DIALIN
- PASS 4 public replies to rotate
- PASS button 'Send the checklist'
- PASS automation disclosed
- PASS human path present
- PASS follow-up at 20h, inside the 24h window (sent only to people who tapped or replied)

## Trigger

- Comment contains **DIALIN** (any case, anywhere in the comment) on these posts only: reel-dialin, carousel-dialin, story-dialin
- One reply per person per post, even if they comment twice.

## Public replies (rotate in order, post after the DM goes out)

1. Sent, check your DMs.
2. In your inbox now. Tell me which step you start with.
3. Done. The checklist is in your DMs.
4. On its way. If it did not arrive, check message requests.

## Messages

1. **First DM** (no link): Here is the dial-in checklist you asked for. Tap below and I will send the link.
   Button: [Send the checklist]
2. **After the tap** (link): Here it is: <tagged link for the placement>
   Start with the 5 checks under Start here, they take about 15 minutes.
   (Automated reply. Write here any time and a person answers.)
3. **Follow-up** at 20h, only to people who tapped but did not open the link: Did the checklist open? Reply with your grinder model and I will send a starting grind setting for it.

## Test before going live

- [ ] From a second account, comment the keyword in lower case inside a sentence. The DM arrives within a minute.
- [ ] Comment a near miss (the keyword with a typo). Nothing is sent.
- [ ] Tap the button. The link message arrives and the link opens on a phone without a login.
- [ ] Reply to the DM with a question. It reaches a person's inbox, not a loop.
- [ ] Comment twice. Only one DM is sent.
- [ ] The public reply appears and does not repeat the previous one.

## Watch weekly

- Comments with the keyword, per placement
- DMs delivered / comments (delivery failures show here first)
- Taps / DMs delivered (the first DM's job)
- Link opens / taps (use the utm_content per placement)
- Next-step actions / link opens (signups, replies, calls)
- Replies a person answered within a day
