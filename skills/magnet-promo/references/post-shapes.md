# Post shapes per platform

Every shape has the same three parts: the hook (the reader's pain in their words), the give-away (one complete item from the magnet), the ask (one keyword or one link, saying what more they get).

## X (280 characters per post)

```
Your espresso tastes different every morning because of 3 things, and 2 of them take 2 minutes to check.

Check 1: weigh the dose every time. A 1 g swing changes the shot more than the grind.

Comment DIALIN and I will send the other 21 checks.
```

For a thread, the give-away becomes 3 to 5 posts, the ask goes in the last one and nowhere else.

## Threads (500 characters)

Same parts, more room for the give-away. A short list works well. Keep the ask on its own last line.

## LinkedIn (3000 characters)

Open with the observation from your own work, in two lines (the preview cuts at about 200 characters). Give the item with the reason behind it. Ask in the last line. Links in the post body can reduce reach; the keyword in comments avoids that.

## Instagram carousel (caption 2200 characters, links not clickable)

Slide outline:
1. The hook as the title, nothing else.
2. to 6. The give-away: one check or step per slide, with the why.
7. "The other 21 checks are in the free checklist."
8. "Comment DIALIN" as the only thing on the slide.

Caption: the hook again, one line of context, the ask. Put the keyword in the pinned comment too.

## Short video script (30 to 45 seconds)

```
0-3s   Hook line on screen and spoken: "If your shots taste different every morning, check this first."
3-25s  Show the one check being done, with the number.
25-35s "That is 1 of 22. Comment DIALIN and I will send you the full checklist."
```

Say the keyword out loud and show it on screen at the same time.

## Telegram channel (4096 characters, 1024 with an image)

Telegram delivers by link or by the bot's start link. Give the item, then one line with the link.

## Newsletter or email

The give-away can be longer here: the item and the story behind it. One link to the magnet. The checker expects exactly one link in a `newsletter` section.

## Pinned comment (keyword platforms)

```
Comment DIALIN for the full 22-point checklist. It arrives in your DMs.
```
